The problem

Why Nazm Dad?

Concentrated Power

Reduce the possibility of durable, unaccountable concentration of political authority.

Institutional Deadlock

Provide continuity and anti-deadlock mechanisms for appointments and governance.

Rights Enforcement

Connect fundamental rights to judicial guarantees and clear limits on public power.

Governance Continuity

Set clear succession and continuity rules for crises and transitions.

Institutional philosophy

Four Fundamental Principles

01

Separation of Powers

Institutional independence of the legislative, executive, and judicial branches.

02

Checks and Balances

No branch is supreme or beyond oversight and accountability.

03

Polycentric Governance

Power distributed among institutions and levels of government without one absolute command center.

04

Unity Through Law

All institutions operate within one shared constitutional framework.

About

An Independent Constitutional Project

Nazm Dad is a constitutional-design project for Iran focused on distributed power, rule of law, territorial self-governance, fundamental rights, and anti-deadlock mechanisms. It is defined as independent, non-partisan, and non-religious.
