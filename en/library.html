Library

The NAZM DAD library brings together constitutional documents, legal frameworks, institutional analyses, and research materials concerning the limits of public power, the effective guarantee of rights, and institutional accountability.

The library is intended to connect constitutional principles with concrete institutional mechanisms, comparative legal analysis, and practical standards for evaluating public institutions.
